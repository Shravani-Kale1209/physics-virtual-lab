About Virtual Lab

The PICT Physics Virtual Lab is an interactive educational platform designed to bridge the gap between theoretical physics concepts and practical laboratory experience.

This platform allows students to visually understand complex physics concepts, perform experiments virtually in a simulated environment, and dynamically vary experimental parameters. By recording observations and analyzing experimental results, students can build a strong foundation and prepare thoroughly before performing physical laboratory experiments.
